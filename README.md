## ⚡ Core Insight

### **In GCC, gravity and inertia are not treated as fundamental forces acting inside a pre-existing spacetime.**

### **They emerge from the response of the discrete substrate to processing load, localization, and information flow.**

Local processing load modifies effective clock rates, spatial intervals, and geodesic motion.

At cosmological scales, redistribution between matter-like and vacuum-like grid sectors can reproduce the standard dark-sector background.

At extreme curvature, saturation of the grid response may replace the classical black-hole singularity with a finite-density regular core.

GCC therefore proposes that dark-matter-like and dark-energy-like phenomena may be **emergent grid-response effects rather than independent fundamental substances**.
